Welcome & stored images

Save an image once in the STU-540, then recall it without another image transfer. It stays in the tablet after unplugging. Enable automatic display in the Connect controls to show it when this app connects.

Boot screen & background

Current settings are read when you connect. The background color resets when the tablet loses power; this page can remember and restore your applied color after it boots.

Firmware boot screen

Connect to read the current boot-screen setting.

This controls the firmware logo shown at power-on. Applying the setting does not restart the tablet or replace its current image. To check it visually, close the connection, finish that confirmation, then unplug and reconnect the cable before reopening the app connection.

Tablet background color

Connect to read the current background color.

Apply a color to remember it and restore it after the tablet boots.

Sets the tablet's clear-screen color and clears its current display for comparison with a solid-color preview. Saved images keep their original colors. This does not change the white margins in prepared welcome images. With restore enabled, the last applied color is saved in this browser and restored before your welcome image on every connection. Keep this page open and enable automatic reopening to restore it after USB reconnection.
